# audio preview (sounds + timeline)

> Client-Facing Documentation  
> Last Updated: 2026-05-08  
> Version: 1.0

## What This Feature Does

This work makes sound editing reliable: you can add sounds to a scene, place them on the timeline, and preview them while you play or scrub.

---

## How It Works

**In Simple Terms:**

1. You attach a sound file to your project.
2. You choose when it should play (a time segment on the timeline).
3. When you press play or move the playhead, the editor plays the sound at the right moments.

---

## Key Benefits

- **Better timing**: preview what users will hear.
- **Faster editing**: quick feedback while scrubbing.
- **Predictable behavior**: clear rules for overlap and looping.

---

## Getting Started

1. Add a sound.
2. Place its segment on the timeline.
3. Press Play or scrub to preview.
